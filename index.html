The PMI Enterprise Partner Hub

A single, closed account surface for organisations that buy from PMI. It merges the administrative functions of the existing Partner Platform with the full PMI B2B portfolio, so an enterprise customer manages the relationship in one place instead of across email, spreadsheets and three portals.

  • Discover and buy — solution catalogue, quotes, agreements and orders
  • Manage — people, access, seats, certifications, cohorts, provisioning
  • Assess — organisation Value Assessment and individual Skill & Ambition Profile
  • Decide — delegated budgets, approval ceilings, escalations, purchase orders
  • Report — utilisation, completion, outcomes, and an exportable enterprise report

Access is by invitation from the account's Capability Sponsor. Every figure in the demonstration build is illustrative.
